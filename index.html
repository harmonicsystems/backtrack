Seconds for each part of one breath. Holds can be 0. A session ends at the end of a cycle.
Swell is how far the Wash ebbs on each exhale. Cues are soft tones in your key: octave to breathe in, fifth to hold, root to breathe out.
B♭ is for trumpet, clarinet and tenor sax, E♭ for alto and baritone sax, F for horn. Note names follow your instrument; the drone plays at concert pitch. Tuning moves the lines and the drone together.
Follow my voice moves the lines up or down an octave to where you're singing. Turn your phone sideways for a bigger line.
